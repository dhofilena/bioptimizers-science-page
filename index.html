Phase Two

The Blueprint

Your body works as a system. So we start by mapping the entire biological chain.

Take blood sugar — it isn't one event. It's a series of connected steps: how your body responds to glucose, how it transports it, and how it puts it to use.

That's why Berberine Breakthrough isn't a single ingredient, but a blend of 10 ingredients designed to support multiple aspects of glucose metabolism.

03

Phase Three

The Hunt

In formulation, everything starts with the raw material. The wrong source, form, or purity, and the rest doesn't matter. Once we know what a formula needs, we look for the best ingredients — not the cheapest or easiest to source. The best.

Our testing goes through two major stages before any ingredient earns a place in a formula.

  1. Stage 01 — Purity & Safety

    We bring in raw ingredients from suppliers around the world and test them using chromatography and mass spectrometry to verify purity, potency, and active compound concentration.

    We screen for heavy metals and conduct genotoxicity and cytotoxicity testing to help evaluate safety at the cellular level.

  2. Stage 02 — Efficacy Testing

    Our team sets up a battery of experiments to answer one question: How well does it actually perform?

    For Magnesium Breakthrough, we ran solubility and stability testing across hundreds of versions — from citrates to bisglycinates — keeping only those with the strongest bioavailability profile.

    Enzymes are measured for catalytic activity using the Bradford assay. Probiotics are evaluated for their ability to survive stomach acid, then grow and form the biofilm that helps them take hold.

04

Phase Four — Our Secret Weapon

Synergy

This is where we separate ourselves from every other supplement company. Where most companies formulate with single ingredients, we engineer interactions: we study cofactors — the molecules that may activate or amplify an ingredient's effect — and we test combinations for synergy.

But synergy doesn't stop inside a single formula. In digestion, stomach acid helps break down food, enzymes help release nutrients, and probiotics help support the gut environment.
